7. Fees and Charges

Dottra applies two charges: a Processing Fee each time you draw, and a Monthly Charge based on the balance you owe during the month. No other fees apply to the credit itself.

The Processing Fee is a one-time fee charged on each Draw. It is deducted from the amount disbursed, so the amount that reaches you is the Draw amount less the Processing Fee.

The Monthly Charge is applied each month to your Outstanding Balance. It is calculated on how much you owed and for how many days you owed it during the month, so repaying earlier reduces the charge.

The current Processing Fee and Monthly Charge rates, together with any minimum amounts and any grace period, are disclosed in the application and on dottra.co before you draw.

The Monthly Charge is not interest that compounds; it is calculated separately for each month on the balance you carried during that month.

There is no membership fee, subscription fee, or registration fee unless explicitly disclosed before you agree to it.

Changes to pricing apply from the date they take effect and to future Draws, and will not retrospectively change charges already applied for a completed period.

Applicable taxes or statutory charges may be reflected in the amounts shown to you where required by law.

Repayments are made through supported payment channels, normally mobile money. Dottra does not collect repayments by direct debit from a bank account.

You may repay all or part of your Outstanding Balance at any time. Because the Monthly Charge is based on the balance you carry during the month, repaying earlier reduces what you pay.

Repayments are applied against your Outstanding Balance according to the allocation method displayed by Dottra and applicable law.

Dottra provides a monthly statement of your Outstanding Balance and the amount due. You are responsible for making payment through a supported channel by the date shown.

If a repayment is not made when due, your repayment obligation remains outstanding and you remain responsible for arranging payment.

As repayments reduce your Outstanding Balance, your Available Credit may increase. Once your Outstanding Balance has been cleared, your full Credit Limit may become available again subject to continued eligibility and account controls.
