Eligibility

Who can apply for Dottra.

Dottra is currently for two groups: people in salaried employment, and government-sponsored students at supported universities in Zambia.

Everyone needs

  • To be 18 or older.
  • A valid Zambian NRC.
  • An MTN, Airtel or Zamtel mobile money wallet registered in your own name.
  • An Android phone, to use the app and take document photos.
  • To pass identity verification: photos of your NRC and a selfie.

Salaried employees

You need to be in salaried employment, in the public or private sector, and able to upload a recent payslip.

In the app you'll add your employer, job title, type of employment, net pay and pay day. We use these, with the payslip, for an affordability review.

As a guide, salaried limits go up to K3,000. Your limit may be lower.

Government-sponsored students

You need to be a government-sponsored student at one of these universities:

  • Chalimbana University
  • Copperbelt University
  • Kapasa Makasa University
  • Kwame Nkrumah University
  • Levy Mwanawasa Medical University
  • Mukuba University
  • Mulungushi University
  • University of Zambia

In the app you'll add your student number and expected graduation year, and upload your student ID and sponsorship document.

As a guide, student limits go up to K400. Your limit may be lower.

How decisions are made

Once your documents are approved, we review affordability and set a credit limit. Meeting the requirements above doesn't guarantee a limit, and limits vary between people. Your limit can be reviewed over time.

Every draw is also checked against your available credit and account status at the time, so having a limit doesn't mean every draw will go through.

Common questions

Can I apply if I am self-employed or run a business?

Not yet. Dottra currently serves salaried employees with a payslip and government-sponsored students at supported universities.

Can students without government sponsorship apply?

Not at the moment. The student route is for government-sponsored students at the supported universities listed on this page.

Does the mobile money number have to be in my name?

Yes. Draws are only sent to a mobile money wallet registered in the same name as your Dottra account and NRC.

Why was my application declined, or my limit lower than I expected?

Limits depend on verified income or sponsorship and an affordability review. We do not set a limit for everyone who applies, and we cannot share the details of how individual decisions are made. You can contact support if you think something was verified incorrectly.
