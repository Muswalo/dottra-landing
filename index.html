Android app, Zambia

A credit facility you can draw from, pay back and use again.

For salaried workers and government-sponsored students. Draw any amount from K10 up to your available credit, receive it on mobile money, and pay back on your own schedule. You get one statement a month.

How it works

Limits are set after identity checks and an affordability review. Not everyone who applies is approved.

How the facility works

What you pay back becomes available again.

  1. 01Get a credit limit
  2. 02Draw what you need
  3. 03Pay back any time
  4. 04Draw again

What it costs

Two charges, both shown in the app before you agree to anything.

01

5% processing fee per draw

Taken from the amount you draw, with a minimum of K1. Draw K500 and K475 reaches your mobile money wallet.

02

20% monthly charge on what you used

Worked out on your average daily balance for the month. Pay back early and the charge is smaller. If you owe nothing, there is no charge.

03

Nothing to join

No registration, membership or subscription fee. Having a limit you don't use costs nothing.

Full pricing and examples

Who it is for

Income that arrives once a month. Costs that don't wait for it.

School fees, transport, a medical bill, stock for a side business. Plenty of people with steady income still get caught between paydays or between allowance payments.

Dottra is for those gaps. It is not meant for long-term borrowing, and it works best when you know what you'll use it for and when you'll pay it back.
