Pricing

Current Dottra pricing.

One rate structure across the current package catalogue, with every figure shown before you confirm in the app.

Catalogue reviewed 12 July 2026. The in-app confirmation is the final source of terms for each draw.

Processing Fee5%

Deducted before the amount is sent.

Fixed Charge20%

Set once when the draw completes.

Term30 days

Due on the date shown in the app.

How the figures work.

The 5% Processing Fee is deducted from the selected amount before payout. The 20% Fixed Charge is added to the selected amount to calculate the total to repay.

For example, select K500, receive K475 in your registered mobile-money wallet, and repay K600 after 30 days.

The Fixed Charge does not grow day by day or compound. There is no registration, membership, or subscription fee.
