Contact

Contact Dottra.

Use the channel that matches your question and we'll get it to the right person.

Help with your account

Use support in the app where you can. It links your message to your account. Otherwise email support@dottra.co or call +260 973 400 223.

Never send your PIN, password, one-time codes or full ID documents by email.

Company and partnerships

For employers, universities, media and other company enquiries, email hello@dottra.co.

Privacy and your data

For questions about your personal data, or to exercise your rights under Zambia's Data Protection Act, email our data protection contact at dpo@dottra.co. Our privacy policy explains what we collect and why.

Complaints

Email support@dottra.co with "Complaint" in the subject line and tell us what happened. We'll confirm we've received it and reply with an outcome. If you're not satisfied with how we handled it, you can contact the Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (CCPC).
